Business Overview
Fig. 6 - Brand Logo EMITRA About
Source: Makosan’s Team
EEMMIITTRRAA is an AI-powered Software-as-a-Service (SaaS)
platform designed to automate the full CBAM compliance
cycle for Indonesian exporters from raw factory document
ingestion to EU-registry-ready XML declaration. The platform
ingests fragmented factory documents such as electricity bills,
fuel invoices, production logs, and material receipts using an
advanced AI-powered OCR engine.
The system calculates Scope 1 and Scope 2 embedded emissions following official EU
methodology, then generates CBAM-compliant XML reports ready for EU Customs Registry
submission, complete with click-through audit trails that trace every calculated number back to
its source document. Customers will purchase EMITRA because of the urgent February 2027
CBAM settlement deadline, the platform's 40–70% cost savings compared to manual
environmental consultants, and its ability to reduce report generation time from weeks to hours
while protecting exporters from punitive EU "default values" that erode price competitiveness.
The unique selling point is clear: from raw factory documents to EU-registry-ready CBAM
reports in hours at less than half the cost of manual audits, with every number traceable to its
source. The single key advantage is cost efficiency with credibility. EEMMIITTRRAA delivers verified,
auditable CBAM compliance at a price point accessible to Tier-2 and Tier-3 manufacturers,
combining AI automation with independent verification partnerships to ensure regulatory
acceptance.
Business Model
EEMMIITTRRAA is an AI-powered SaaS platform that automates the full CBAM compliance cycle for
Indonesian exporters from raw factory document ingestion to EU-registry-ready XML
declaration. EMITRA's business model operates as a B2B enterprise SaaS provider that
integrates AI-powered document processing, deterministic emission calculation engines, and
independent verification partnerships. Platform development is carried out through an internal
technology team that builds OCR and AI extraction systems, EU methodology-compliant
calculation engines, and XML report generators. Client acquisition is conducted through
industry association partnerships (IISIA, ALFI, KADIN) and direct enterprise sales to
factory management in industrial estates. Services are then distributed through B2B
channels such as industrial associations, direct enterprise sales, and inbound marketing.

Emitra Feature Showcase
1. AI-Powered Document Extraction
EEMMIITTRRAA transforms physical export documents,
invoices, certificates of origin, bills of lading — into
structured digital data automatically. Powered by a
LayoutLM model specifically trained on international
trade documents, our system understands not just the
text, but also the spatial context and relationships
between fields on each document. What once required
hundreds of hours of manual data entry per year is now
completed in seconds.
2. Human-in-the-Loop Validation
AI speed without human accuracy is simply a new kind
of risk. EEMMIITTRRAA implements a Human-in-the-Loop
mechanism: when the AI's extraction confidence score
falls below 90%, the document is automatically routed
to a validator for side-by-side review — the original
document on one side, the extracted data on the other.
The validator only needs to correct specific fields, not
start from scratch.
The result: 99%+ accuracy without sacrificing speed
3. EU CBAM Emission Calculation
EU CBAM regulations demand precise,
methodological emission data. EEMMIITTRRAA's calculation
engine computes three core components:
Scope 1: direct emissions from production processes
Scope 2: indirect emissions from consumed electricity
Embedded Intensity (total emissions per unit of output)
All calculations strictly follow EU methodology,
producing figures that are not only accurate, but also
prepared for scrutiny from European Union authorities.

Emitra Feature Showcase
4. Automated XML Report Generation
The EU Registry emission report format is highly
specific — a single formatting error can result in
submission rejection. EEMMIITTRRAA eliminates this risk by
automatically generating XML files that are 100%
compatible with EU Registry standards. No manual
spreadsheet conversion. No incorrect templates.
Simply click download, and the file is ready to be sent
to European Union authorities.
5. Multi-Company Compliance Dashboard
For environmental consultants managing dozens of
exporters simultaneously, tracking each client's
compliance status is a real challenge. EEMMIITTRRAA's
dashboard presents everything in a single view: each
company's CBAM compliance status, export volumes,
total recorded emissions, and submission deadlines.
Each client card features a progress ring that provides
an instant overview — no need to open individual
reports one by one.
6. Cost Savings Comparison
Manual emission audit costs range from USD 75,000–
150,000 per year — not including the risk of human
error and EU Registry rejection. EEMMIITTRRAA offers a
solution starting from IDR 30 million per year, with
equal or greater accuracy thanks to the combination of
AI processing and human validation. Savings of up to
67% are not just a number — this is a demonstrable
ROI that can be presented to every potential client.

Conclusion
EEMMIITTRRAA is an AI-powered SaaS platform that addresses the structural compliance
crisis created by the European Union's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism
(CBAM), which threatens Indonesia's USD 757.7 million in annual exports of iron,
steel, and aluminum to the EU. By automating the full CBAM compliance cycle from
raw factory document ingestion to EU-registry-ready XML declaration. EEMMIITTRRAA
delivers 40–70% cost savings compared to manual environmental audits, transforming
carbon compliance from a financial burden consuming up to 14% of annual net profit
into an efficient and affordable process. With the urgent February 2027 CBAM
deadline approaching, EEMMIITTRRAA targets 24 clients by Year 3 and 320 cumulative
regional clients across ASEAN by Year 5, with financial break-even projected at
3Y2M and revenue reaching up to IDR 2 billion by Year 3.
EEMMIITTRRAA driven by a strong management team, strategic partnerships with industry
associations (IISIA, ALFI, KADIN) and verification bodies (SUCOFINDO, SGS),
