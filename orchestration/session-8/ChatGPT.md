## Brand Positioning

Emitra = "The Bridge Between Indonesian Exporters and the EU Carbon Frontier"
- Bukan green company biasa — ini **export enabler**
- Trustworthy (data compliance), Intelligent (AI-native), Indonesian pride

---

## Logo Concepts

### Concept A: Geometric — RECOMMENDED ✅
- Bentuk segi enam (hexagon) merepresentasikan molekul karbon
- Tersusun dari panah ke atas melambangkan **growth + sustainability**
- Warna dua gradasi: emerald + navy
- Typeface: Inter bold, clean sans-serif
- **Alasan pilih:** Paling versatile, tampil profesional di dokumen resmi dan digital

### Concept B: Organic
- Bentuk daun dengan garis sirkuit — natural + tech
- Terlalu generik, mirip banyak logo green startup

### Concept C: Monogram
- Inisial "E" dengan aksen carbon molecule
- Minimalis, tapi kurang storytelling

## Brand Voice

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Professional** | B2B enterprise tone — no jargon, no fluff |
| **Urgent but calm** | CBAM is urgent, but Emitra makes it manageable |
| **Indonesian pride** | "Dibangun untuk eksportir Indonesia" as recurring theme |
| **Data-driven** | Use numbers, not adjectives |

### Example Taglines
1. "Navigating CBAM, Empowering Exporters" (formal)
2. "Your Passport to EU Carbon Compliance" (short)
3. "Emitra: Karena Ekspor Indonesia Tidak Boleh Berhenti" (Indonesian — untuk pasar lokal)

---

## Visual Guidelines for Frontend

- **Layout:** Clean cards, generous whitespace — dashboard style
- **Typography:** Inter (UI), system monospace (data/tables)
- **Icon style:** Outline, consistent stroke width (2px), rounded corners
- **Data viz:** Charts should use emerald → navy gradient for carbon data
- **Trust signals:** Badge "EU CBAM Compliant", SSL padlock icon, audit trail icon
